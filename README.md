### Hello, I'm racklore 👋

Hey, I'm racklore. I spend most of my time writing Python, JavaScript, and Go — usually building CLI tools, dev workflows, and small utilities that make the day-to-day a little smoother. I'm drawn to minimalism in software: less config, fewer moving parts, more focus. I contribute to open-source projects whenever I can, and I'm a bit obsessive about developer ergonomics — my Neovim and Docker setups have seen more iteration than most of my actual projects.
